**Classification:** Politics

**Body:**

Georgia elects its Supreme Court justices in officially nonpartisan races — no party labels on the ballot. But the endorsements, the donors, and the networks behind the candidates are all on the public record, and they are anything but neutral.

We put the receipts on cards: match what's on the record to the candidate it belongs to, and every correct match reveals the source.

Judicial races get a fraction of the attention of everything else on the ballot. Does the "nonpartisan" label inform voters — or shield them from information they'd want?
